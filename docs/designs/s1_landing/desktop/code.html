"Your nominee sees nothing until a death is verified."

We understand privacy and cultural sensitivity. Your nominated family members cannot see your cover amounts, policies, or personal notes during your lifetime. Access is strictly unlocked only when a death is validated via the Department of Home Affairs or certified BI-1663 documentation.
