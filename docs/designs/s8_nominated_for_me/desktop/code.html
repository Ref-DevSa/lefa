The Trusted Person Role

In South Africa, thousands of funeral policies go unclaimed because families don't know policy numbers during bereavement. Lefa protects your dignity.

1

What you can see now

Only confirmation of who nominated you. No monetary sums, policy documentation, or banking data. Complete family privacy.

2

When someone passes

Tap “Report a death” to upload the doctor or hospital BI-1663 death notice. Lefa validates it with Home Affairs within hours.

3

Immediate bereavement access

Direct access to policy schedules so funeral parlours (AVBOB, transport, repatriation) can be arranged without out-of-pocket family stress.
